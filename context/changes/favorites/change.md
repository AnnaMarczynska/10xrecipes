## Notes

Authenticated users can save recipes from search results to a persistent favorites list, view their favorites, and remove recipes from the list. Favorites persist across sessions and include metadata (recipe ID, name, timestamp).

Completion of S-02 (user auth) and F-04 (data layer) unblocked this work.
